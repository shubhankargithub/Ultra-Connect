About Ultra Connect

Ultra Connect helps schools and educators take care of everything around education — so they can focus on what matters most: teaching and learning.

Who We Are

You Focus on Education. We Handle Everything Around It.

Ultra Connect is a complete education transformation solutions company. We coordinate the full school ecosystem — technology, academics, infrastructure, safety and student experience — through a single, trusted partner.

Instead of managing many different vendors, a school works with one coordinated team. One platform connects the digital backbone of the school; one partner brings the campus and the classroom experience together. That is what “One Partner. One Platform. Complete School Transformation.” means.
