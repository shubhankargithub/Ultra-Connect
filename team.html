Our Team

The People Behind the Transformation.

A coordinated group of specialists across education technology, academics, infrastructure, safety and campus experience — working as one partner for every school.

One Coordinated Team

No Fragmented Vendors. One Accountable Team.

Ultra Connect brings together specialists who would otherwise be separate vendors. Each area of the school — technology, academics, campus, safety and student experience — is owned by a team that already works alongside the others.

That is what lets a school plan once, order once and be supported once, instead of coordinating a dozen suppliers. Our team stays with your institution from the first visit to long after implementation.
